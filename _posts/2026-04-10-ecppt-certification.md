## eCPPT - Certified Professional Penetration Tester

**Issuer:** INE / eLearnSecurity  
**Date Earned:** April 2026  

### About This Certification

The eCPPT (Certified Professional Penetration Tester) is an intermediate-level, fully practical certification. It validates the ability to conduct professional-grade penetration tests, including advanced techniques like network pivoting, buffer overflow exploitation, and comprehensive web application attacks.

### Skills Validated

- ✅ Advanced Network Penetration Testing
- ✅ Network Pivoting & Tunneling
- ✅ Buffer Overflow Exploitation (32-bit)
- ✅ Web Application Attacks (SQLi, XSS, CSRF, etc.)
- ✅ Post-Exploitation & Privilege Escalation
- ✅ Professional Report Writing

### Exam Format

- **Type:** Practical / Hands-on
- **Duration:** 14 days (7 days testing + 7 days reporting)
- **Environment:** Multi-network lab requiring pivoting
- **Deliverable:** Professional penetration test report
